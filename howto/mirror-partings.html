Mirror Partings¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

Copy a parting onto the other side of the head, check it, then keep it or throw it away.

You Need First

  • Place Partings
  • A head with no Mirror modifier. If yours has one, see Work On A Head With A Mirror Modifier instead.

Steps¶

  1. In the parting list, select the parting you want to mirror.
  2. Select the mirror button beside the list. A pending mirror appears on the other side.
  3. Look at the mirror on the head.
  4. Select the mirror button again.
  5. Select Confirm (make permanent) to keep it, or Deactivate (destroy mirror) to remove it.

Mirror While You Place¶

You can also mirror as you go. Press M while placing, and each parting you place gets a pending mirror. Press M again to stop.

Deal With Several Pending Mirrors¶

Pending mirrors are counted under the list heading. Select Confirm All to keep them all, or Deactivate All to remove them all.

Don't leave them pending for long. With more than five, the panel warns you. Apply Bake and Freeze Layer stay greyed out until none are pending.

If You're Stuck

  • Looking for an old mirror toggle in the sidebar? See I Cannot See The Mirror Toggle In The Sidebar.
  • Partings doubled on a mirrored head? See Partings Are Doubled, Or Land In The Wrong Place, On A Mirrored Mesh.
