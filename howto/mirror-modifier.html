Work On A Head With A Mirror Modifier¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

Place and bake partings on a head that uses a Mirror modifier. Nothing gets doubled or lost in the bake.

You Need First

  • Place Partings

Don't Mirror Twice

If your head has a Mirror modifier, don't also mirror partings with the add-on. Leave M off while placing.

Steps¶

  1. Select your head mesh.
  2. Open the Modifier properties.
  3. On the Mirror modifier, leave Realtime (the screen icon) on.
  4. Turn Render (the camera icon) off.
  5. Place partings on the real half of the head only.
  6. Select Enable Preview to see how they land on the mirrored half.
  7. Select Disable Preview before you bake.

A parting can sit across the mirror seam. That is how you get a centre part down the middle of the head.

If the Mirror modifier is still on for renders, the Bake box shows a red warning. See Mirror Modifier Warning.

If You're Stuck

  • Partings doubled or in the wrong place? See Partings Are Doubled, Or Land In The Wrong Place, On A Mirrored Mesh.
