Straight Vs Premultiplied Alpha¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

Two ways of storing colour in a see-through PNG. Leave Premultiplied off unless your game engine asks for it.

Overview¶

Straight alpha (Premultiplied off) keeps the full colour in every pixel, and stores how see-through it is separately. Even fully transparent pixels keep their colour. When an engine shrinks the texture for distance, the wrong colour can't bleed in at the edges.

Premultiplied alpha (Premultiplied on) multiplies the colour by the transparency before saving. Some engines and pipelines expect this. In those, it gives correct edges. Elsewhere it gives up the protection straight alpha has.

The setting only affects the file Save Transparent writes. It has nothing to do with the hidden pass the bake makes for the transparent export. That pass is always used and has no switch.

If You're Stuck

  • A pale edge round the partings? See The Transparent Export Has A Pale Or Skin-Coloured Edge.
