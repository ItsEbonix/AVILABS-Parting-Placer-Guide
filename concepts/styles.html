Styles¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

A style is a complete set of partings on one head. Styles let you keep several looks on the same head and switch between them.

Overview¶

The first style on a head is called Style 1. New Style… parks the current set and gives you an empty one to place into.

Only one style shows on the head at a time. The others are parked, and their partings are kept in your file. Their Empties are hidden in a collection named after the head and the style, for example head - Style 1.

Each style keeps its own baked maps, so baking one style doesn't touch another's.

Selecting a parked style loads it and parks the one that was showing. Turn Preview off before you switch.

If You're Stuck

  • Partings missing after switching? See I Cannot See Any Partings.
