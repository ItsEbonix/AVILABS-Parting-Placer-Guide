Live, Preview And Frozen Partings¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

A parting can be in one of three states. Knowing which is which explains most viewport slowdowns.

Overview¶

State What it means How it gets there
Live Drawn from scratch by Blender every time the material updates. Quick to edit, slow in large numbers. Every parting starts live.
Preview Shown as a low-resolution stand-in. Fast, and nothing is lost. Enable Preview, or Auto Preview when you start placing.
Frozen Baked into a checkpoint and taken out of the material for good. Bake Partings, then Freeze Layer.

The number of live partings is what slows Blender down. Each parting adds its own block of shader nodes for Blender to process.

Preview is the one to reach for first. It costs nothing, and Disable Preview brings full shading back.

Freezing is for partings that are settled. You can freeze 3 times per material, and frozen work stays in your maps however many times you bake after.

Baking alone does not change any state. After Bake Partings, every parting is still live.

If You're Stuck

  • The viewport is slow? See The Viewport Is Slow.
  • Preview stayed on? See Preview Mode Is Still On After I Finish Placing.
