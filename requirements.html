Requirements And Compatibility¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

Check this page before you install, and again before you change Blender version.

What You Need¶

  • Blender 4.4 or 5.2. See Compatibility for other versions.
  • A mesh head with a material and a UV map.
  • Material Preview or Rendered viewport shading. Partings are invisible in Solid shading.

The UV map matters most when you bake. Baking writes into the head's UV layout, so the bake cannot run without one.

Compatibility¶

Blender Status
4.4 Tested on 4.4.3, October 2026
4.5 Untested
5.0 Untested
5.1 Untested
5.2 Tested on 5.2.2, October 2026

Untested means nobody has confirmed the add-on works on that version yet. Keep a backup of your file if you try it.

A file saved in Blender 5.2 does not open in Blender 4.4. If you move between versions, keep a copy saved from 4.4.

Finding Your Add-on Version¶

Open Edit > Preferences > Add-ons and find Parting Placer Tool in the list. The version is shown there.

The i at the left of the panel title shows it too. See Info Button.

If You're Stuck

  • No partings showing? See I Cannot See Any Partings.
  • Bake button greyed out? See Bake Partings Is Greyed Out Or Refuses.
