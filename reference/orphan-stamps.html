Orphan Stamps Warning¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

Every parting is placed by an Empty in your scene. If that Empty is deleted by hand, the parting is left behind with nothing to place it. The panel calls that an orphan stamp and shows a warning box at the very bottom.

You won't see this box unless something has gone wrong.

Orphan Stamps Warning¶

The box counts the orphans and says what is wrong with them, for example:

2 stamp(s) have no driving Empty

Projected in world space — placement is wrong.

An orphan stamp no longer sits where you placed it.

Discard Orphan Stamps¶

The button in the warning box, for example Discard 2 Orphan Stamp(s). It deletes every orphan stamp on the head. Your other partings are not touched. The status bar reports how many it removed.

If You're Stuck

  • A parting has gone missing? See I Cannot See Any Partings.
  • Anything else: Still Stuck.
