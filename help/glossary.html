Glossary¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

Short definitions of the words this guide and the panel use.

Parting¶

One parting image placed on the head. Each one is an Empty plus a shading effect in the head's material. See How A Parting Is Built.

Stamp¶

The panel's word for one parting, as in Stamps: 2 in the key guide or Live · 2 stamp(s). It means the same as parting.

Empty¶

A Blender object with no mesh. Each parting has one, and it sets where the parting sits and which way it faces. Moving the Empty moves the parting.

Pending Mirror¶

A mirrored copy of a parting that you haven't kept yet. Confirm it to keep it as a parting of its own, or deactivate it to remove it. See Mirror Partings.

Preview Proxy¶

The low-resolution stand-in that Preview Mode shows in place of your live partings. The status line calls it cached, as in Preview · 2 stamps cached at 1024px. See Preview.

Frozen Layer¶

A set of partings baked into a checkpoint by Freeze Layer and taken out of the material. Frozen work stays in your maps. You can have 3 per material.

Coverage Map¶

The README's name for the Alpha map. It shows where your partings are, not what colour they are.

Style¶

A complete set of partings on one head. One style shows at a time, and the rest are parked. See Styles.

Partings Material¶

The copy of your head's material that the add-on works on. Its name ends in _Partings, for example Material.001_Partings. Your original material stays in the file.

Live Bump¶

The switch at the bottom of the Appearance box that shows parting relief in the viewport. It starts off. Turn it on before you bake a Normal map. See Live Bump.

Parting Mask¶

The image each new parting is stamped with. Pick it in the Parting Mask box. See Parting Mask.

Edit Warp¶

A way to bend a parting by dragging handles in the viewport. See Edit Warp.

Lattice¶

A warp with a grid of handles, from 2 x 2 up to 8 x 8. A corner warp has 4 handles. See Convert To Lattice.

Soft Radius¶

How far a drag in Edit Warp pulls nearby handles along. At 0 only the handle you hold moves. See Soft Radius.

Fold Guard¶

Stops a lattice warp from folding back through itself. See Fold Guard.

Names You See In The Outliner¶

The add-on adds a few things to your scene. You can leave them alone.

Name What it is
PARTING_Empty_000, PARTING_Empty_001 … One Empty per parting
Partings.head The collection holding the Empties, named after your head
head - Style 1 Where a parked style's Empties are kept
PARTING_Mirror_Root.head A helper for mirroring, named after your head
