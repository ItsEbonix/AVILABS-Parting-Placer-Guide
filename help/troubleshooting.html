Troubleshooting¶

Applies to: Parting Placer V2.0.0 · Blender 4.4 · 5.2

Find your symptom below. Each entry gives the fix first, then the reason.

I Cannot See Any Partings¶

Switch the viewport to Material Preview or Rendered shading. Press Z to pick one. Partings are shading, so they don't show in Solid.

Already in Material Preview? Check these:

  • The parting isn't hidden. Each one has an eye in the Parting List.
  • The panel is working on the right head. See Target.
  • The parting isn't on the far side of the head. Orbit round before you assume it failed.
  • You switched styles with Preview on. See Style.

A Parting Looks Doubled Or Folded¶

Update to V1.3.1, run Repair Partings once, then look at the parting again.

A parting with strongly moved corners could show a faint second copy of itself, folded back over the first. Partings already in a file keep their old look until you run Repair once.

Partings Look Flat, Or The Bump Sliders Have Gone¶

Turn on Live Bump. The switch is at the bottom of the Appearance box. See Live Bump.

From V2.0.0 it starts off. Bump is the slowest part of the material for Blender to rebuild, so placing stays quick without it. Bump Strength and Bump Distance show again while it is on.

The switch covers every partings material in the file, including parked styles and other heads.

Partings Turn Magenta In Material Preview¶

Update to V2.0.0, run Repair Partings once, then look again.

On some graphics cards, a head carrying about 25 partings could turn them magenta. Every parting's warp now shares one image, so the count no longer runs into the limit. Repair moves partings already in a file over. Its message counts the warp tile node(s) added.

Place Parting Does Nothing¶

Select your head mesh, or set it as Target. Make sure the head has a material.

Then move the mouse over the head. The preview only follows the cursor while it is over the head.

If the add-on can't start, the status bar says why. See Place Parting for each message.

Bake Partings Is Greyed Out Or Refuses¶

Check these, in order:

  1. Preview is on. Select Disable Preview first.
  2. The head has no UV map. Unwrap the head first.
  3. The head has no partings. Place at least one.
  4. Only Normal is ticked and Live Bump is off. Turn on Live Bump, or tick another map too.

Pending mirrors don't block Bake Partings. A dialog asks whether to include them. See Include pending mirrors in bake.

My Bake Has No Normal Map¶

Turn on Live Bump, then bake again.

With Live Bump off there is no bump to bake. The Normal tick box is greyed out, and the bake skips it. If Normal was the only map ticked, the bake stops with a warning.

Freeze Layer and Apply Bake warn you in their confirm box when the last bake had no Normal map. See Apply Bake.

The Face Shows Through My Baked Normal Map¶

Update to V1.3.0 or later, run Repair Partings once, then bake again.

Older versions let the bump reach outside the partings. Eyes, nose, lips and ears showed up in the normal map. Until you run Repair, the file looks unchanged. That is expected.

Partings Bake With Small Holes In Them¶

Raise Samples in the Bake box.

The default of 16 leaves around 0.4% of the parting area holed. Past 16 the gains fall off sharply while the bake gets slower.

Dark Speckles Or Short Dashes Inside My Partings After Baking¶

Update to V1.1.0 or later, then bake again.

The dashes only show in the baked maps and the transparent export, never in the viewport. Maps baked with an older version keep them until you re-bake.

Baking Takes Much Longer Than It Used To¶

This is expected from V1.1.0. The old speed came at the cost of dropped pixels in every map.

To work faster, bake at a lower Resolution while you work, and raise it for the final pass. Keep Normal Resolution high, since it carries the fine detail.

The Baked Colour Is Darker Than My Hair Color Swatch¶

Lower Detail. At 0 the bake matches your swatch.

Detail darkens the parting, and the bake shows your colour after Detail is applied. The hue stays the same. Only the brightness changes.

Partings I Froze Have Disappeared From My Maps¶

Update to V1.1.0 or later. From then on, frozen partings survive any number of later bakes.

Before V1.1.0, freezing then baking more than once lost frozen partings from the Alpha map. The Color, Normal and Roughness maps were never affected. Alpha already lost can't be recovered. Re-place and re-bake those partings, or carry on and let your next freeze start fresh.

Freeze Layer Is Greyed Out¶

Check these, in order:

  1. You haven't baked yet. Select Bake Partings first. Freeze Layer reuses that bake.
  2. Mirrors are pending. Confirm or deactivate them.
  3. You've used all 3 freezes on this material. The limit can't be raised. Start a new style instead.

My Baked Maps Vanished¶

The usual cause is File > Clean Up > Purge Unused Data. Bake again to get them back.

Some maps are kept on the material without being plugged into anything. Blender thinks they are unused and deletes them. The Alpha map, the transparent-export pass and every frozen snapshot are in that group. A lost frozen snapshot can't be re-baked, because its partings are gone from your scene.

Before you purge, open the Image Editor and switch on the shield icon (fake user) for those maps. Saving your file is safe, because the maps are packed into it.

Partings Are Doubled, Or Land In The Wrong Place, On A Mirrored Mesh¶

If your head has a Mirror modifier, don't also mirror partings with the add-on. You would be mirroring twice.

Place partings on the real half only, and turn the Mirror modifier off for renders. See Work On A Head With A Mirror Modifier.

The Viewport Is Slow¶

Select Enable Preview. Nothing is committed, and Disable Preview brings full shading back.

Then close the Shader Editor if it is open. For partings that are settled, bake and use Freeze Layer. See Keep The Viewport Fast.

Preview Mode Is Still On After I Finish Placing¶

This is on purpose. The partings you just placed stay live on top of the stand-in, so they are still quick to adjust. Select Disable Preview when you want full shading back.

Preview Mode is saved with the file. A file saved with it on opens with it on.

Dashed Lines Run From Every Parting To The Head¶

Select the Relationship Lines icon in the panel header. It hides the lines in that viewport and changes nothing in your file.

The Transparent Export Has A Pale Or Skin-Coloured Edge¶

Bake once more with V1.1.0 or later, then export again.

The export now takes its colour from a pass that leaves the skin out. A soft edge keeps the parting's own colour. A material last baked with an older version falls back to the old look. The status bar message after saving tells you which was used.

16-bit Export Looks The Same And The File Is Much Bigger¶

Switch back to 8-bit unless you see banding in the colour of a soft edge.

16-bit helps the colour, not the transparency. Transparency is stored at 8-bit either way, in a file about three times the size. If the banding is in the edge's transparency, 16-bit won't help.

Save Transparent Is Unavailable¶

Tick Alpha in the Bake box and bake again. Save Transparent needs both Color and Alpha baked, and Alpha is off by default.

The Key Guide Has Disappeared From The Viewport¶

Look along the top of the viewport. If the guide can't be drawn, it shows there as a single line for the rest of the session.

To get the full guide back, turn the add-on off and on again in Edit > Preferences > Add-ons. This doesn't touch your partings, styles or baked maps.

If it keeps happening, email the console text to help@ebonix.com. On Windows, open it with Window > Toggle System Console. On macOS and Linux, start Blender from a terminal.

Ctrl+Z Crashed Blender After I Placed Partings¶

Update to V1.3.0 or later.

Now, one Ctrl+Z after you finish undoes the whole session. Ctrl+Z while placing still removes one parting at a time.

I Cannot See The Mirror Toggle In The Sidebar¶

It was removed in V1.2.0. Press M while placing to turn mirroring on and off. Partings you already mirrored are unaffected.

The Depth Slider Has Gone¶

It was removed in V1.2.0 and is fixed at 0.5. Partings placed earlier keep the value they had.

An Appearance Slider Will Not Go As High As It Used To¶

Each parting's sliders now stop at the same limits as the shared ones. Opacity stops at 100.

If a parting was set past a limit, Repair Partings brings it back to that limit. Check anything you pushed far on purpose before you run Repair.

Still Stuck¶

Email help@ebonix.com with:

  • your Blender version
  • the add-on version, from Edit > Preferences > Add-ons
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened
  • a screenshot of the panel and of the problem

If the problem is in a baked map or an export, attach the image itself.
